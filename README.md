# E-Commerce SQL Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the design and analysis of an E-Commerce database using MySQL. The database stores information about customers, products, categories, orders, order items, and payments. A total of 45 SQL queries were written to perform business analysis and extract actionable insights from the data.

---

## Objectives

- Design a relational database for an e-commerce business.
- Practice SQL concepts from basic to advanced level.
- Perform customer, product, sales, and revenue analysis.
- Generate business insights using SQL queries.

---

## Database Schema

The project consists of the following tables:

### Customers
Stores customer information.

### Categories
Stores product categories.

### Products
Stores product details, pricing, and inventory.

### Orders
Stores customer order information.

### Order_Items
Stores products included in each order.

### Payments
Stores payment details for orders.

## Key Business Insights

1. Total revenue generated by the business was **₹103,895**.

2. **Aditya Sharma** placed the highest-value order worth **₹82,998**.

3. **iPhone 15** was the most expensive product priced at **₹79,999**.

4. **iPhone 15** generated the highest revenue among all products.

5. **Aditya Sharma** was the highest-spending customer.

6. **Bluetooth Headphones** recorded the highest quantity sold.

7. **Electronics** was the best-performing category by sales volume.

8. **Aditya Sharma (₹82,998)** and **Sneha Gupta (₹12,000)** were the only customers who spent more than ₹10,000, forming the premium customer segment.

9. **iPhone 15 (₹79,999)** and **Samsung Galaxy S24 (₹74,999)** were the only products priced above the average product price.

10. **iPhone 15 (₹79,999)**, **Microwave Oven (₹12,000)**, and **Bluetooth Headphones (₹5,998)** generated the highest revenue, contributing a major share of total sales.
